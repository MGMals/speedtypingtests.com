Improve Your Typing Skills

Improving your typing speed is about more than pressing keys faster. Strong typing combines speed, accuracy, proper finger placement, muscle memory, and consistent practice.

Beginners should focus on accuracy before trying to increase WPM. As your fingers become more familiar with the keyboard, your typing speed can improve naturally.
