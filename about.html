About SpeedTypingTests

SpeedTypingTests is a free online platform designed to help students, professionals, and job seekers improve their typing speed, accuracy, and confidence through interactive typing practice.

What Is SpeedTypingTests?

SpeedTypingTests provides free typing speed tests that allow users to measure their words per minute (WPM), typing accuracy, and overall performance.

Whether you are learning keyboard skills, preparing for a job, improving productivity, or simply challenging yourself, our typing tools are designed to make practice simple, effective, and enjoyable.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make typing practice accessible to everyone. We believe typing is an important digital skill that can improve communication, productivity, education, and career opportunities.

By providing free typing tests and engaging practice experiences, SpeedTypingTests helps users build stronger keyboard skills at their own pace.

How Our Typing Tests Work

Improving typing speed starts with consistent practice. Our typing tests allow users to:

  • Choose a typing test duration
  • Type the displayed words and sentences
  • Receive instant WPM and accuracy results
  • Identify mistakes and improve over time
